\begin{abstract}
A spatial holdout is a choice of estimand, not a degree of strictness: withholding a target location while retaining nearby, contemporaneous or grid-sharing sources asks whether a model interpolates within an observed network; removing those pathways asks whether it transfers beyond it. The two can return opposite verdicts on the same data. We develop a data-adaptive audit that removes these pathways in turn, each control set from the measured dependence structure, not from convention. On CAMS \pmten{} residual correction across 19 stations, three conventions each independently flip the verdict: setting the temporal guard from the measured autocorrelation scale ($+0.19$ at three days, $+0.08$ at ten) removes the gain at all radii, equal-count blocks correct a fold imbalance of 124 to 1.01, and an aggressive flat-line filter restores an apparent gain. Because an empirical null cannot separate suppression of a false claim from destruction of a real one, we also run a 32-scenario stochastic experiment with signal and each dependence pathway set by construction. Across 25-, 50- and 100-km buffers the full audit holds false-positive verdicts near 2--3\% on average, rising to 11--13\% in one corner where a common temporal component makes the station-level sign test anti-conservative. Specificity is bought with power: under the transparent corrector, coordinate-free models retain 35--37\% detection and coordinate-bearing models 22.5--27.8\%, a decline a gradient-boosted tree does not reproduce although it matches the specificity. A validation design should be reported with the pathways it closes and the trade-off it incurs, not ranked by strictness.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Data availability}
The aligned three-hourly observation--model records underlying every empirical value reported
here are available in the public \texttt{serra\_camscast} repository,
\url{https://github.com/bisu9082/serra_camscast}. The raw CAMS EAC4 fields are distributed by
the Copernicus Atmosphere Data Store and are not redistributed. The repository does not ship a
retrieval client; the exact request parameters needed to rebuild the aligned records from that
archive --- dataset, variables, grid, time grid, unit conversion and the station-matching rule ---
are documented in \texttt{data\_retrieval/RETRIEVAL.md}. Ground observations are from the OpenAQ
open-data archive.

\section*{Code availability}
All code underlying this article is in the same public repository under the MIT licence:
the empirical audit scripts and their result artefacts, the figure scripts, and the
ground-truth stochastic experiment (\texttt{serra\_simulation\_v2.py}) together with its
structural tests, its pre-declared configuration and the 25-, 50- and 100~km Monte Carlo
outputs for both correctors. The version reported here is release \texttt{v1.1}, commit
\texttt{2c22d1fdcadf839bf43263634c744f5e7e0489a9}. In a fresh clone of that release,
\texttt{smoke\_test.py} recovers the 20,818 matched three-hourly records and passes its
integrity checks, the simulation structural tests pass, and \texttt{agg\_learner.py}
reproduces the three-radius learner comparison reported in Supplementary Table~S43, including its direct paired tests, while \texttt{empirical\_learner\_paired.py} reproduces the paired learner comparison of the empirical audit. Every figure in the main text is
regenerated from the deposited result artefacts by \texttt{make\_fig\_audit.py}
(Figures~\ref{fig:guard} and \ref{fig:blocks}), \texttt{make\_fig5.py}
(Figure~\ref{fig:checks}) and \texttt{make\_fig\_simulation.py}
(Figures~\ref{fig:simladder} and \ref{fig:simradius}), each run with the figure directory
emptied; the two supplementary figures are deposited as rendered images only. \texttt{paired\_effect\_sizes.py} recomputes the paired effect sizes of the coordinate contrast from the deposited per-station deltas.
